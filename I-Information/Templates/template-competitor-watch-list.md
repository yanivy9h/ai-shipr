## What to Flag in Weekly Scan

- New product features or UI changes
- New partnerships (payer, employer, distribution)
- Funding rounds or acquisitions
- Executive hires or departures (VP+)
- Pricing changes or new pricing pages
- Shifts in primary messaging or target audience
- New content series or SEO topic clusters
- Negative review themes that your product could address
