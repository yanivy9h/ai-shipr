# Stakeholders

The named people you must align with to ship anything. Keep this list short — 3 to 7 — and refresh it when the surrounding context changes (new exec, reorg, project pivot).

---

## Rubrics

Every ordinal field below has a defined scale. Agents must reference these rubrics when assigning a level. If a field needs a level outside this rubric, propose extending the rubric — do not invent a private scale.

### Trust level with product team

How much credit the stakeholder extends to product judgment without re-litigating it. Anchored in observed behavior over the last 1–2 quarters, not on personal rapport.

- **High** — accepts product recommendations with light context. Pushes back on substance, not on principle. Defends product calls in rooms you're not in.
- **Medium** — accepts recommendations when the reasoning is shown end-to-end. Re-opens decisions when surprised. Defensive when initiatives slip.
- **Low** — re-litigates roadmap decisions. Requires evidence at every step. Routes around the PM to engineering or design. A "Low" rating triggers a relationship-repair plan, not just better communication.

### Decision role

Which slot the stakeholder occupies for the *current* initiative. A stakeholder can be Sponsor on one initiative and Informed on another — re-assess per initiative.

- **Sponsor** — owns the outcome, has the budget, can kill or fund the work.
- **Approver** — must say "yes" for the initiative to proceed (legal, security, exec sign-off).
- **Influencer** — shapes the decision through judgment or coalition, but doesn't sign off.
- **Informed** — needs to know, doesn't need to approve.

### Current sentiment toward our work

Direction of pressure on the current scope, not personality. Refresh after every major review.

- **Champion** — actively advocates for the initiative outside the room.
- **Supportive** — agrees with direction, won't actively push for it.
- **Neutral** — no strong position; will follow the room's consensus.
- **Skeptical** — has unresolved concerns but is engaging in good faith. Treat as a leading indicator — Skeptical drifts to Blocker if concerns aren't addressed within ~2 weeks.
- **Blocker** — actively opposed, raising it in rooms you're not in. Requires a direct one-on-one before the next milestone.
