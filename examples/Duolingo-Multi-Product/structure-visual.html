The shared/ rule: If a decision constrains or connects both products, it belongs in shared/. If it's local to one product, it stays in that product's folder. When the Head of Engineering says "one major platform change this quarter" — that's a Constraint in shared/, not in either product strategy.

Single Product vs. Multi-Product — What Changes

Setting / File Single Product Multi-Product
Settings.md team_mode: solo team_mode: lead
Strategy files One S-Strategy/ folder One per product + shared/ layer
Stakeholders One Stakeholders.md Per product — some overlap (e.g. iOS Lead appears in both)
/today output Single product morning brief Surfaces priorities across both products + portfolio conflicts
Populate-Strategy Fills one product Fills per-product layers + generates shared/ automatically
Portfolio-Roadmap.md Not used Lives in shared/ — combined initiative timeline
